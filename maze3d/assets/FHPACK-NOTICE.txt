dev/lib/apple2/lz4fh.asm is the ca65 translation of LZ4FH6502.S with scratch
addresses configurable through caller scratch aliases. It preserves the decoder's
instructions and uses the hidden HGR page as its destination.
The compressor is invoked with -9 -h to preserve all 8192 source bytes.
