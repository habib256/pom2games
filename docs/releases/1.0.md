# MICRO-SOKOBAN 1.0 — Apple II+

The first release of MICRO-SOKOBAN is a bootable DOS 3.3 disk image for the Apple II+ with 48 KB RAM and later Apple II models. Download `MICRO-SOKOBAN.dsk` from this release, mount it as a 5¼-inch disk, and boot it. The image includes the game and its level collections; no installation is needed.

The game contains 454 puzzles from David W. Skinner's Microban I–IV collections: 150, 122, 92, and 90 puzzles respectively. Puzzles that fit when rotated are rotated; puzzles that cannot fit the 20 × 12 playfield or clear its HUD corners are omitted. Original collection and puzzle numbers remain visible in the game.

- Colour hi-res graphics, keyboard and joystick controls, and five introductory puzzles.
- Undo and redo with a 1,024-move history, restart, and direct level selection.
- Ten independent player profiles, saved records, and saved puzzle positions. A changed position is saved when opening the menu or after about six seconds without input.
- A Hall of Fame ranked by puzzles solved, then by the fewest moves in each player's best results. The HUD shows both moves and pushes.
- Optional sound and dead-square warnings, plus an automatic demonstration.

Use **I/J/K/L**, **W/A/S/D**, the arrow keys, or a joystick to move. Press **U** to undo, **Y** to redo, **ESC** or joystick button 1 to save the current position and open the menu, and **H** for help. An Undo/Redo history remains available during a session; after a reboot, the saved board and counters return with a fresh history.

All 454 included puzzles and the five tutorial puzzles were played through in automated tests on an Apple II emulator. Profile isolation, saving and rebooting, write-protected disks, and the menu were also checked. Real-hardware feedback is welcome.

Puzzle collections: **David W. Skinner**, distributed with credit. Apple II adaptation: **Arnaud Verhille**, based on the POM1 Apple-1/GEN2 Sokoban sketch. [Source code](https://github.com/habib256/pom2games/tree/1.0/micro-sokoban) is available under the repository's GPL-3.0 licence.
