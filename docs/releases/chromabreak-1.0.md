# ChromaBreak 1.0 — Apple //e and //c

ChromaBreak is a free, open-source brick-breaker in double hi-res for the enhanced Apple //e (128 KB) and the Apple //c. Download `CHROMABREAK.po`, mount it as a 5¼-inch disk and boot it: it starts ProDOS and the game. A 65C02 is required.

## Features

- Double hi-res in 16 colours, 30 frames per second (25 on PAL machines)
- Sixty boards, with tiles that take one, two or three hits, and steel
- Six bonus capsules: Enlarge, Slow, Catch, Disrupt, Laser and Pierce
- Enemies, combos up to ×8, extra lives
- Mouse, keyboard, joystick or paddles; the paddle also moves up and down
- Two-voice music on the built-in speaker
- Three difficulty levels, high scores and progress saved on the disk
- A help page in the game: press `?` on the title
