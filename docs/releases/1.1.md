# MICRO-SOKOBAN 1.1 — Apple II+

MICRO-SOKOBAN 1.1 is a bootable DOS 3.3 disk image for the Apple II+ with 48 KB RAM and later Apple II models. Download `MICRO-SOKOBAN.dsk`, mount it as a 5¼-inch disk, and boot it. The disk contains all 454 Microban I–IV puzzles and the five introductory lessons; no installation is needed.

## What's new since 1.0

- **Title music:** a quiet, swung melody played through the built-in speaker. MENU SOUND controls it; the automatic Hall of Fame and demonstration stay silent by default.
- **Solution playback:** enable CHEAT MODE in OPTIONS to reveal SOLUTION in the menu. It plays the current puzzle's solution, then restores your position without recording a result. Playback can be interrupted.
- **Monochrome readability:** crates on goals now have a hollow green frame and a white check mark. All seven gameplay tiles have different silhouettes even without colour.
- **Title presentation:** centred “APPLE II PORT BY” / “VERHILLE ARNAUD” credits, white profile and V1.1 labels on black panels, and a slower corridor animation. The animation finishes with the crate on its goal before the ten-second Hall of Fame presentation.
- **Navigation and resume fixes:** canceling level selection from the title returns to the title; RESUME retains the saved position; RESTART from the title starts the level with zero counters.
- **Display and exit fixes:** menus and solution playback restore the correct board and video page. QUIT TO DOS and Ctrl-RESET restore the BASIC launcher, so LIST and RUN work again.

## Playing

Use **I/J/K/L**, **W/A/S/D**, the arrow keys, or a joystick to move. **U** undoes, **Y** redoes, **ESC** opens the menu, and **H** opens help. The menu provides profiles, restart, level selection, options, the Hall of Fame, and exit to DOS.

The game retains ten independent player profiles, saved puzzle positions and records, and a 1,024-move Undo/Redo history during play. After reboot, the saved board and counters return with a fresh history. Puzzle collections remain unchanged: 150, 122, 92 and 90 puzzles from David W. Skinner's Microban I–IV, keeping their original collection and puzzle numbers.

## Validation and credits

The release disk was rebuilt and compared byte-for-byte with the committed image. Automated emulator tests cover all 454 puzzles, the five tutorial lessons, solution playback, title music, video-page restoration, menu navigation, saved positions, profiles, ranking, write-protected media, and return to BASIC. These checks validate emulated Apple II execution; they do not replace testing on real hardware.

Puzzle collections: **David W. Skinner**, distributed with credit. Apple II adaptation: **VERHILLE Arnaud**.
