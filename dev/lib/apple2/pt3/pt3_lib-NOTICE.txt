Local corrections in pt3_lib_core.s
(2026-11-03): use PT3_DISABLE_FREQ_CONVERSION for noise as for tones and
envelopes; preserve the full 20-bit product when scaling a 16-bit envelope;
omit redundant tone loads when conversion is disabled; fix amplitude slide
bounds/zero crossing, persist vibrato countdowns, and preserve signed sample
envelope offsets without adding the sign carry. The internal
pt3_emit_registers label allows exhaustive register-conversion tests.
pt3_zp.inc replaces
upstream zp.inc (same layout, base PT3_ZP instead of $60) and pt3.s replaces
the IRQ handler (decode only: no Mockingboard writes, no IRQ installation,
no slot patching, no //c ROM work-around).

Optional fixed-note-table specialization (2026-11-06): pt3.s and
pt3_lib_init.s omit unused table generators for a declared immutable table,
verify its header at init, and produce silent/no-op playback on mismatch
until a matching reinitialization. The default general configuration emits
identical bytes to the prior local version. No upstream license text changes.
